# Three minutes of GODMODE

## Before going on

1. Open the public HTTPS URL on the presenting computer. Create a fresh universe. Keep that creator tab open.
2. Scan the QR on an actual phone, enter a nickname, and send one rule. Confirm the name and effect on the projector.
3. Reset the room. Return the phone to the controller; participants stay connected through reset.
4. Check projector legibility and use fullscreen. Keep the QR sidebar visible during joining. Sound is optional and off initially.
5. Choose a player: one teammate on WASD/Space, or Guided run if you are presenting alone. Keep the computer and tunnel awake if using temporary hosting.

## 0:00–0:25 — Give away the power

“This is Nova. Nova wants to get home. Unfortunately, we gave every one of you godmode. Scan this code.”

Point to the QR and the live gods-online count. Let people join. They can use a nickname or join without one. The UI gives them immediate rule buttons.

## 0:25–0:50 — Make it personal

Start the round. Move and jump while explaining the three energy cells.

“Someone make the floor lava.”

Wait for a real audience member to do it. Their name appears, the floor changes, and Nova must stay off it. If the room is slow to join, the presenter can explicitly demonstrate the same rule using the sidebar. Do not imply that a presenter-generated rule came from an audience member.

## 0:50–1:25 — Create a rescue

“Okay. Someone else save us.”

Point out the help powers: shield, bridge, healing, slow motion, trampoline. React to what actually happens. People now understand that they can cooperate as well as sabotage.

## 1:25–2:10 — Escalate

“You can combine things. Try moon gravity and rain ducks.”

The room submits real combinations. Low gravity changes the astronaut and objects; ducks and boulders bounce through the same arena. Let the names and overlapping powers tell the story. If the scene gets cluttered, use Clear chaos and immediately ask for a new combination.

## 2:10–2:40 — Show the mechanism in one sentence

“Your words change a running simulation. Familiar rules apply immediately, and compatible rules stack. There’s no build step between your phone and the world.”

The optional AI interpreter is only worth mentioning if a provider has actually been configured and verified. The base experience is honest and complete without it.

## 2:40–3:00 — Finish deliberately

If the player has collected all three cells, reach the rocket for the earned win. Otherwise say, “Let's end this universe properly,” and use Launch finale. This is a presenter-triggered ending, separate from winning the mission.

“One screen. Every phone. The audience is the game.”

## Recovery controls

- **Pause:** stops the round and temporarily blocks new commands; resume preserves the remaining time on active effects.
- **Clear chaos:** removes live world effects and dynamic objects.
- **Reset:** clears the mission and rule history while retaining the room code and connected audience.
- **Guided run:** a slower automatic tour with pauses at fuel cells; manual movement takes over on WASD/arrows.
- **F:** fullscreen. **H:** hide/show sidebar and presenter controls. **P:** pause/resume.

Use the local/LAN URL as a network fallback only if audience devices can reach that address. Always generate the QR from the origin that the phones should actually use.
